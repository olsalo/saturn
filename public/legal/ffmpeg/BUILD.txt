The complete corresponding source is included here as ffmpeg-8.0.1.tar.xz.
Build on macOS 15+ with Apple command-line tools for the target architecture:
First build libaom, libvpx and Opus using their adjacent BUILD.txt files. Set PKG_CONFIG_PATH to all three lib/pkgconfig directories (colon-separated), and use pkgconf 2.3.0 or compatible.
tar -xf ffmpeg-8.0.1.tar.xz
cd ffmpeg-8.0.1
./configure --disable-autodetect --disable-everything --disable-network --disable-doc --disable-debug --disable-shared --enable-static --disable-ffplay --enable-ffprobe --disable-x86asm --enable-ffmpeg --enable-videotoolbox --enable-libaom --enable-libvpx --enable-libopus --enable-audiotoolbox --enable-protocol=file,pipe --enable-demuxer=mov,matroska --enable-indev=lavfi --enable-muxer=mp4,webm,null,rawvideo --enable-decoder=prores,h264,hevc,aac,pcm_s16le,pcm_s24le,pcm_s32le,pcm_f32le,rawvideo,wrapped_avframe,libaom_av1,libvpx_vp9,opus --enable-encoder=hevc_videotoolbox,h264_videotoolbox,libaom_av1,libvpx_vp9,libopus,aac_at,wrapped_avframe,rawvideo --enable-parser=h264,hevc,aac,av1,vp9,opus --enable-filter=scale,format,fps,aresample,aformat,anull,null,color,psnr --extra-cflags=-mmacosx-version-min=15.0 --extra-ldflags=-mmacosx-version-min=15.0 --pkg-config-flags=--static
make -j8 ffmpeg ffprobe
No patches. Linked statically with libaom, libvpx and Opus; their complete source, copyright and patent notices accompany this file in adjacent directories. See scripts/prepare-ffmpeg.mjs for packaging.
