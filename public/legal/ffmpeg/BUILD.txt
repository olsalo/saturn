FFmpeg 8.0.1, an unmodified, separate LGPL-2.1-or-later executable bundled with Saturn.
Source: https://ffmpeg.org/releases/ffmpeg-8.0.1.tar.xz
SHA-256: 05ee0b03119b45c0bdb4df654b96802e909e0a752f72e4fe3794f487229e5a41
The complete corresponding source is included here as ffmpeg-8.0.1.tar.xz.
Build on macOS 15+ with Apple command-line tools for the target architecture:
tar -xf ffmpeg-8.0.1.tar.xz
cd ffmpeg-8.0.1
./configure --disable-autodetect --disable-everything --disable-network --disable-doc --disable-debug --disable-shared --enable-static --disable-ffplay --disable-ffprobe --disable-x86asm --enable-ffmpeg --enable-videotoolbox --enable-audiotoolbox --enable-protocol=file,pipe --enable-demuxer=mov,lavfi --enable-indev=lavfi --enable-muxer=mp4,null --enable-decoder=prores,h264,hevc,aac,pcm_s16le,pcm_s24le,pcm_s32le,pcm_f32le,rawvideo,wrapped_avframe --enable-encoder=hevc_videotoolbox,aac_at,wrapped_avframe --enable-parser=h264,hevc,aac --enable-filter=scale,format,fps,aresample,aformat,anull,null,color --extra-cflags=-mmacosx-version-min=15.0 --extra-ldflags=-mmacosx-version-min=15.0
make -j8 ffmpeg
No patches or external libraries. See scripts/prepare-ffmpeg.mjs for packaging.
